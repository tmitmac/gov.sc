Ministry of Labour & Employment

Discover the Ministry of Labour’s initiatives, policies, and programs that protect workers’ rights, promote fair employment, and ensure safe workplaces.

Ministry Overview

The Ministry of Labour & Employment oversees employment standards, workplace safety, and labour market growth. Its mission is to support fair wages, worker protections, and economic opportunity across all sectors.

Leadership

  • Minister of Labour & Employment: Hon. Layton Cox
  • Secretary of Labour: Georgia Sunderland

Key Focus Areas

  • Workplace health and safety enforcement
  • Monitoring and ensuring fair wages
  • Employment law compliance and dispute resolution
  • Job training and workforce development
  • Supporting equality and diversity in employment
