Election Voting

Use this to find information on voting requirements and practices, the election timetable, and to cast an online vote in the 2026 General Election.

Eligibility

  • You must be a Square Country resident or citizen
  • You must provide valid identification
  • Previous licence is required for renewals

Required Information

  • Full Name
  • Citizenship ID
  • State of Registry
  • Candidate (and party) Choice

Where can I vote?

Anyone eligible to vote can vote in-person, via mail-in, or online.

Voting in Elections

Important: Comisión Electoral | Electoral Commission of Square Country is responsible for overseeing matters relating to local, regional, and national elections in Square Country. Click the button below to go to the Electoral Commission website.

All citizens of Square Country, aged 18 years and above, are eligible to vote in all levels of elections. Under Square Country law, voters who are eligible to vote must cast their ballot in any election.

Under the Electoral (Online Voting) Amendment Act 2026, voters are now able to cast their ballots online, as opposed to in person at designated polling booths.

Voting has opened for the 2026 General Election. Before you vote, ensure you have your full name, citizenship number, electorate, and choice of vote.
